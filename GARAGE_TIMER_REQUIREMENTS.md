# Garage technician timer rules

- The daily lunch reminder is at **1:00 PM**.
- A second reminder appears at **1:55 PM**, warning that the lunch pause ends and the technician's timer resumes at **2:00 PM**.
- The finishing-time reminder follows the configured workshop closing time (currently 6:00 PM).
- A technician starts the timer on their first assigned job of the day.
- A technician cannot stop a job timer.
- Switching jobs requires a recorded reason, including vehicle finished, needs parts, needs a test drive, needs attention, or a custom reason.
- Only the Workshop Manager can stop a timer, and a reason is mandatory.
- Each technician may take one timer pause of up to one hour for lunch each day.
- The system reports when each technician started their first job of the day.
- A technician may transfer onto another technician's job to help. Their time stops counting against their previous job and starts counting against the assisted job.
- Every active technician on a job consumes labour allowance simultaneously. Two active technicians make the remaining labour allowance count down at twice the normal rate.
- The assisted job card shows every technician currently counting time against it.
- When a helper returns to their own job, their labour time transfers back.
- Every start, switch, lunch, assistance period, return, manager stop, reason, and timestamp is retained in the audit history.
